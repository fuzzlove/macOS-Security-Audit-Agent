# Compliance feature-gap register

The machine-readable register is `COMPLIANCE_FEATURE_GAP_REGISTER.json`. P0 items are complete verified catalogs/objectives, contract and scope modeling, and official scoring/POA&M rules. P1 is the structured SSP/shared-responsibility workflow. P3 is complete Rev. 3 transition content. These are `PLANNED` or `RESEARCH`, not implemented. Manual fallbacks keep assessment work outside MSAA explicitly incomplete rather than fabricating results.
