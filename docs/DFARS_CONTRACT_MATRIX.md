# DFARS contract matrix

The applicability record must separately capture FAR 52.204-21 and DFARS 252.204-7008, -7012, -7019, -7020, -7021, and -7025, clause date, flowdown, owner, evidence, deadlines, provider obligations, status, gap, and source. Presence in this registry never proves inclusion in a specific contract.

For -7012, MSAA prepares but never submits the 72-hour DIBNet record, malware material, or government package. Discovery, submission, and 90-day preservation times remain distinct.
