# Anti-Ransomware Legal and Safety Boundary

MSAA is a defensive tool for a local device the operator is authorized to protect. It is not a law-enforcement tool, does not bypass access controls, capture credentials, covertly surveil users, or exfiltrate telemetry. File contents are excluded from evidence by default.

Observe and Confirm Before Containment are the safe defaults. Stronger modes require explicit local confirmation. MSAA never automatically deletes files, silently elevates privileges, or blindly terminates system processes. Installation is a visible headless administrator operation; the GUI must run as the logged-in user.

Results describe observed facts separately from analyst hypotheses. “Mapped to,” “aligned with,” “readiness support,” and “evidence support” do not mean certification, compliance, government endorsement, guaranteed prevention, or legal advice.
