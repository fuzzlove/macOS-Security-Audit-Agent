# Process Injection Benign and False-Positive Review

Candidates are never automatically false positive. False-positive, authorized-instrumentation, and expected-administration dispositions require an authorized reviewer, reason, and supporting case/evidence reference. Observations remain immutable and decisions remain separate.

Benign-context records require publisher/signer, owner, approval, expiry, reviewer, expected primitives, and evidence; production deployments should additionally constrain hashes, versions, paths, process pairs, rights, users, roles, and maintenance windows. A match lowers maliciousness only. Deviations are displayed and require reopening/re-evaluation.

Suppressions must be narrow, expiring, owned, reviewed, versioned, and auditable. Broad wildcards require elevated authorization. Occurrences and representative evidence remain. Health, loss, policy, and integrity events are not ordinary detection suppressions.
