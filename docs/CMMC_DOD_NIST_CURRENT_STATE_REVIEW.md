# CMMC, DoD, and NIST current-state review

Verified 2026-07-10 from primary government sources. MSAA is an assessment-readiness and supporting-evidence tool, not a certification or official assessment system.

## Current implementation

The pre-existing CMMC module contains 11 illustrative endpoint-evidence mappings: 3 Level 1, 7 Level 2, and 1 Level 3. It does not contain the complete 15/110/24 catalogs or complete assessment objectives. Its former percentage and half-credit formula was not an official methodology and has been disabled. Local macOS checks provide potential technical evidence only; governance, policy, physical, personnel, training, supplier, interview, and organizational test evidence remain manual.

Current strengths include local-first collection, HTML/JSON output, optional Office output, technical evidence mapping, no-certification disclaimers, and Python 3.14 frozen operation. Material gaps are complete official catalogs, objective procedures, scope/asset/CUI models, official scoring weights, POA&M eligibility, reviewer workflow, SSP, provider responsibility, supplier flowdown, and professional report conformance.

## Verified baseline

- Phase 1: 2025-11-10 through 2026-11-09.
- CMMC Level 1: 15 FAR 52.204-21 requirements; annual self-assessment and affirmation; no POA&M.
- CMMC Level 2: 110 NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 requirements; Level 2 Assessment Guide 2.13; Rev. 2/2018 assessment procedures remain the contractual track.
- CMMC Level 3: final Level 2 (C3PAO) prerequisite for the same scope plus 24 selected February 2021 SP 800-172 requirements.
- CMMC Hashing Guide: 2.14, September 2025.
- SP 800-171/171A Rev. 3: final 2024-05-14, future-readiness only.
- SP 800-172/172A Rev. 3: final 2026-05-13, future-readiness only.

## Evidence classification

Host collectors can provide direct evidence only for a narrowly technical objective when scope, authenticity, integrity, relevance, recency, completeness, and reviewer disposition are established. Otherwise evidence is supporting, contextual, insufficient, stale, contradictory, out of scope, or unverified. No endpoint result establishes an organizational requirement by itself.
