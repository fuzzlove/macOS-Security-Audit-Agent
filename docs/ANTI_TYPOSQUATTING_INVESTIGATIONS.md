# Lookalike Investigation Controls

Machine analysis may assign only Unreviewed Lookalike, Owner Verification Pending, or Suspicious and Requires Further Investigation. Probable Impersonation, Confirmed Fraudulent, report submission, and registry-action states require an identified human reviewer and rationale.

Confirmed Fraudulent additionally requires authoritative evidence: registry enforcement, a verified owner statement, legal determination, validated incident-response evidence, or authorized malicious-package analysis. Similarity, download counts, age, or maintainer display names are insufficient.

Every transition records prior state, new state, actor, human/machine origin, rationale, and UTC timestamp. Registry reporting is prepared locally for human review and is never submitted automatically.
