# Process Injection Novelty and Lineage

The engine evaluates target acquisition, foreign modification, execution transfer, provenance mismatch, and post-injection effects. Partial sequences remain evidence. Each template comparison records shared, missing, different, and contradictory primitives plus similarity and mapping status.

A partial near-template graph becomes a variant. A graph satisfying defensible invariants without adequate template coverage becomes `NOVEL_PROCESS_INJECTION_CANDIDATE`. Sequential internal IDs use `MSAA-PI-YYYY-NNNN`; they are not ATT&CK, CVE, CWE, CAPEC, or government identifiers. Novelty states begin `TRIAGE_REQUIRED`. Promotion, merger, rejection, reproduction, or rule validation requires human research.

Footprint similarity uses structured primitives and always states that similarity does not establish malware, tool, actor, organization, or government identity. No external publication or attribution is automatic.
