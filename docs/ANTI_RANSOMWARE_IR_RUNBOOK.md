# Anti-Ransomware Incident Response Runbook

Organization policy: ransom payment is prohibited. MSAA provides no payment, negotiation, cryptocurrency, attacker-contact, or attacker-decryptor workflow.

1. Confirm and scope the incident without assuming one signal proves ransomware.
2. Isolate affected systems under approved policy while retaining required management access.
3. Preserve volatile and durable evidence and record sensor limitations.
4. Protect unaffected systems, backup credentials, repositories, and recovery media.
5. Identify affected identities, disable compromised sessions, and rotate credentials from a clean system.
6. Identify persistence and affected volumes; eradicate through an authorized plan.
7. Validate clean, tested recovery sources. Do not restore while active compromise remains.
8. Rebuild and restore in priority order, then monitor for reinfection.
9. Prepare reports for configured internal leadership, legal/privacy, insurance, CISA, FBI/IC3, or other authorized contacts. MSAA never transmits them automatically.
10. Record decisions and conduct post-incident review.
