# Standards version policy

New sources never activate automatically. Activation requires official-domain verification, document and pack hashes, schema/count/identifier/cross-reference validation, human review, migration impact, tests, and explicit approval. Content packs retain current, previous, and rollback identities. Historical assessments keep their original profile and hashes.

Statuses are `CURRENT_CONTRACTUAL`, `CURRENT_REGULATORY`, `CURRENT_GUIDANCE`, `FINAL_FUTURE_READINESS`, `DRAFT_WATCH`, `SUPERSEDED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `HISTORICAL`, and `NOT_VERIFIED`.
