# NIST Revision 3 transition

SP 800-171/171A Rev. 3 became final 2024-05-14. SP 800-172/172A Rev. 3 became final 2026-05-13. They are isolated future-readiness profiles. A complete transition pack must identify split, merged, added, removed, and materially changed requirements; depth/coverage; ODPs; evidence reuse; new evidence; and engineering dependencies. This pack is not yet complete.
