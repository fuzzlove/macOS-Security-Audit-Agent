MSAA does not merely report that a security control is configured. MSAA produces fresh, tamper-evident evidence showing whether the control is configured, operating, observable, tested, and recoverable.

The native assurance engine evaluates those dimensions independently. A pass needs
fresh passing evidence for every required dimension and healthy required collector
heartbeats. Exceptions remain excepted, simulation remains non-production, and
silence becomes unknown or degraded rather than success.
