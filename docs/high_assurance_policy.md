# High-Assurance Policy Profile

`STANDARD`, `ENTERPRISE`, and `HIGH_ASSURANCE` are policy profiles, not certification claims. High Assurance selects stricter scoped authorization, longer retention, mandatory High/Critical acknowledgment, shorter reconciliation intervals, required sensor and evidence-chain health, privacy-minimized notification text, and restricted suppression. Broad maintenance windows remain prohibited; dual approval is a deployment option.

Useful mappings include NIST Cybersecurity Framework Detect/Respond, SP 800-53 AU/CM/SI controls, SP 800-61 incident handling, SP 800-92 log management, CIS Controls, the CIS Apple macOS Benchmark, and MITRE ATT&CK. These mappings support assessment and do not establish government approval or compliance certification.
